Questions drivers ask

Before you book

What are the benefits of window tinting my vehicle?

Automotive window tint helps reduce heat and glare, blocks harmful UV rays, protects interior surfaces from fading, and improves overall driving comfort. It can also add privacy and enhance the appearance of your vehicle.

Is window tint legal in my area?

Window tint laws vary by state and are based on visible light transmission (VLT) percentages. A professional installer will ensure your tint meets local regulations while still delivering maximum performance.

How long does automotive window tint last?

High-quality window tint can last many years when properly installed. Premium films are designed to resist bubbling, peeling, and fading, often backed by manufacturer warranties.

How much does window tinting usually cost?

The cost of window tinting depends on the type of film, the size of the vehicle or building, and whether the project is automotive, residential, or commercial. A professional installer can provide an accurate quote based on your needs.

How long does the install take, and what do I do after?

Most cars are done in about an hour. Leave the windows up for three to five days while the film cures. Light haze or small water pockets are normal during curing and disappear on their own.
